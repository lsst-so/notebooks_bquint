**Terminology wrinkle, documented not changed.** RTN-093 (`criteria.tex`) labels its 0.45″
survey-start criterion "sDIQ" but says "The system contribution for purposes of this calculation is
obtained by subtracting quadrature the estimate of the atmospheric contribution obtained from the
wave front sensor donut analysis. This approximation thus excludes the dome seeing." Under Phil's
definitions that is an iDIQ-like quantity, which is why the goal-box top sits on the iDIQ axis.
README §4 and §6 say so and keep RTN-093's own name when quoting it.
