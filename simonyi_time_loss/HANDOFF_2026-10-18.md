## 4. Caveats to carry into any talk or paper text

- Cloud is invisible to the EFD criteria. Before 2026-02-25 the weather number rests on telemetry for humidity, dew
  point, wind and condensation, and on the night reports for cloud (18 nights). After that date the WEATHER bit is
  itself an observer judgement.
- Non-weather loss bundles fault, idle, planned engineering off sky (e.g. 2025-11-06, 2026-03-04, 2026-06-01),
  earthquake inspections (2026-03-13), visitor demos (2026-04-19) and staffing (2026-07-11/12). "Fault" in the talks
  is Bruno's simplification.
- The narrative log and `rubin_nights` use different fault definitions (handoff §7); the comparison in notebook 05 is
  indicative only (correlation 0.6 both ways).
- On-sky time counts the dome-open exposures; image quality is not assessed. 2026-04-24 (6.5 h of corrupted images,
  telescope on sky) is counted as on sky.
