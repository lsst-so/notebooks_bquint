## Caveats

- No mention ≠ no weather: the notes only mention weather when it was notable; many OPEN nights may have had partial cloud or humidity.
- Monday notes compress three nights (Fri/Sat/Sun); for 2026-03-27/28/29 the note gives no per-night breakdown, so the weekend's tickets are listed on all three rows and the status of each night is inferred from "Mostly a good weekend".
- Hours lost are quoted only when stated in the note (0.45 h on Mar 1; 2.1 h + >2 h delay on Mar 2; 9 min on Mar 3; ~30 min on Mar 8; ~3 h on Mar 9; ~15 min on Mar 14; ~1 h workstation on Mar 16; ~4 h on Mar 26). PARTIAL nights without hours (Mar 6, 7, 13, 17, 19, 24, 31) have an unquantified loss.
- The Mar 19 "early closing" has no closing time stated; it is marked PARTIAL, not CLOSED.
- Mar 4 is CLOSED because of planned maintenance/upgrades, not a fault; P(fault) is kept low to avoid counting it as unplanned loss.
- AuxTel weather and fault closures are noted in evidence but never used to judge Simonyi.
- Note authors and note quality vary (some notes report "didn't catch details"); the Night Operations section is the only part used for status judgements.
