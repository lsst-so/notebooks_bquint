Source: nightlog_simonyi_20251025_20260720.csv (export from the rubin-night-log reports via USDF, 2026-10-18). 28 nights; a report exists for every day_obs in the month (no UNKNOWN nights). Same columns as the tailgate files; "note (link)" is the Nightly Digest link for that day_obs. Times are UTC as written in the report (CLT = UTC−3 in February). Simonyi was off-sky from Feb 1 to Feb 15 because of the LSSTCam cryo-vacuum leak (FRACAS-338); the dome was opened on most of those nights for dome venting/thermal tests only, which is not on-sky time.

| status | Simonyi on-sky status judged from the report: OPEN / PARTIAL / CLOSED / UNKNOWN (report missing or silent). |

| P(fault) | Judged probability, 0–1, that lost time was fault/technical (incl. power glitches, flagged). "?" when UNKNOWN. |
| evidence | Verbatim or near-verbatim text from the report; clock times when given. |
| tickets | OBS keys listed in the report. |

Loss that is neither weather nor fault (staffing, planned work) has both P at 0 and the reason in the evidence.

## Summary for this window

- **Weather closures/losses:** 2 full nights — Feb 20 and Feb 21, both dew point / condensation on the Dynalene pipes and TMA (09:10 h lost each, per the report). Feb 25 partial start delay from dew-point constraints (overlapping with dome errors). Feb 19 had clouds in the west from 05:00 UTC with <1 mag extinction but observing continued. High humidity on Feb 1–3 would likely also have prevented opening, but the stated cause those nights was the camera.
- **Fault closures:** 16 full nights — Feb 1–15 (LSSTCam cryo-vacuum leak, FRACAS-338; dome venting/thermal tests only, Feb 15 was camera recommissioning / SuperBias) and Feb 17 (camera firmware issue after the daytime loss of all four glycol chillers, FRACAS-344). Fault losses on open nights: Feb 16 (≈1 h AOS misconfig), Feb 18 (≈20 min dome), Feb 19 (≈1.75 h mount + alignment), Feb 22 (0.6 h camera), Feb 23 (≈20 min CCW), Feb 24 (≈30 min CCW/dome), Feb 25 (≈2 h dome open + ≈2 h CCW; PARTIAL), Feb 27 (1.34 h dome/mount/camera), Feb 28 (unquantified mount/scheduler/alignment).
- **Neither (staffing/planned):** Feb 3 night crew left ~06:30 UTC (staffing, backup OS exhaustion) — irrelevant to Simonyi time since the camera was already down. Feb 15 partly planned camera recovery.
- **Power glitches:** Feb 3 (dinner-time power cut, glycol/dynalene off ~1 min, OSS fault), Feb 11 (~5 min cut at start of night), Feb 26 (08:12 and 08:57 UTC, generators ran), Feb 27 (daytime). Earthquake 6.1–6.2 mag on Feb 12 daytime, systems recovered.
- **UNKNOWN nights:** none.
- Totals: OPEN 9 (Feb 16, 18, 19, 22, 23, 24, 26, 27, 28), PARTIAL 1 (Feb 25), CLOSED 18 (Feb 1–15, 17, 20, 21).

## Caveats

- Status is on-sky status: dome-open nights for venting/thermal tests (Feb 4–14) are CLOSED because no science or on-sky engineering images were taken.
- On the camera-leak nights the reports write "8hrs time loss" (or ~9 h); these are the reports' figures, not an astronomical-night computation.
- Feb 1–3 weather (RH >70–80%) overlapped with the camera outage; P(weather) is kept low because every report names the camera as the reason. Feb 25 weather and the dome fault overlap at the start of the night; both probabilities are given.
- Feb 15 is judged CLOSED with P(fault) 0.8: the night was consumed by camera recovery after the leak plus a camera fault, partly planned.
- No hours are stated for Feb 26 and Feb 28 losses; Feb 28 includes alignment repeats attributed to AOS strategy, not weather.
- Single-source table (night reports only); no Slack/Confluence cross-check here.
