| status | Simonyi on-sky status judged from the report: OPEN / PARTIAL / CLOSED / UNKNOWN (report missing or silent). |

| evidence | Verbatim or near-verbatim text from the report; clock times when given. |
| tickets | OBS keys listed in the report. |

Loss that is neither weather nor fault (staffing, planned work) has both P at 0 and the reason in the evidence.

## Summary for this window

- **Weather closures / losses:** none. No night in November was closed or shortened primarily by weather. Weather-influenced losses are partial at most: 2025-11-03 (clouds/scattered moonlight degraded data, dome stayed open, switched to bad-seeing FBS ~04:10–07:30 UTC) and 2025-11-08 (humidity 66% contributed to closing early near the end of the night, alongside an OSS failure). 2025-11-06 was a planned closed-dome day, but the report also describes 100% humidity, full cloud, −2 °C and snow, so the night would not have been observable regardless.
- **Fault closures / losses:** 12 PARTIAL nights, all attributed to faults — 11-01 (≈3 h MTAOS), 11-05 (TMA/GIS interlock delaying on-sky prep, ≈0.5 h M1M3TS after power glitch), 11-08 (≈2 h mount/encoder + OSS), 11-10 (2.5 h OSS), 11-11 (2–2.5 h Rapid Analysis/quota), 11-18 (≈2 h Cryo 6 PLC), 11-20 (≈ closed: 4.5 h TMA + camera, only initial alignment on sky), 11-22 (≈1 h camera jgroups), 11-23 (≈ closed: 6.5 h recovery after power glitch, ~30 min FBS), 11-25 (unquantified start delay, Dynalene flow + alignment config), 11-27 (1.5 h + ~1 h, power outage then TMA/dome/GIS), 11-30 (4 h TMA encoders). Four OPEN nights also carry quantified fault losses under 1 h: 11-17 (45 min FES), 11-21 (≈45 min TMA/OSS + camera), 11-28 (≈0.75 h MTScheduler). Rough quantified fault total ≈ 34 h over the month, excluding 11-25.
- **Neither (staffing / planned):** 2025-11-06 — planned closed-dome calibration night (mirror cleaning, CBP/monochromatic flats).
- **Power glitches (counted under fault, flagged):** 11-05 (morning glitch, generator; second three-phase glitch ~02:25 UTC), 11-20 (30 s glitch at 15:05 local on generator→grid switch; Dynalene flow lost, residual WFS HV issue at night, FRACAS-321), 11-23 (commercial power outage shortly after 00:02 UTC; 6.5 h recovery), 11-27 (10 min commercial outage, 1.5 h lost), 11-28 (daytime brief cut, lights only, no impact). Also 11-19/11-20 the summit ran on the Mitsubishi generator (planned, no loss stated).
- **UNKNOWN nights:** none — all 30 day_obs have a report.

## Caveats

- Single-source table from the night reports only; the tailgate (Source 1) file for Oct/Nov 2025 should be cross-checked, in particular 11-03 (cloud degradation vs loss), 11-07 and 11-25 (unquantified delays).
- Hours in "≈" are taken from the reports' own estimates or from two clock times in the report; they are not telemetry-derived. The ≈34 h fault total in the summary is a rough sum of those figures only.
- Status convention used here: OPEN = on sky for the night with interruptions under ~1 h; PARTIAL = a stated loss of ~1 h or more, or an unquantified delay the report calls significant; "PARTIAL (≈ closed)" = nearly no science data. P values for OPEN nights with a quantified sub-hour fault loss are set to 1.0 (attribution), with the magnitude in the evidence; small unquantified interruptions get 0.1–0.5.
- 2025-11-07: the report's "initial alignment ~ 21:25 UTC" is inconsistent with the 23:01 UTC OSS fault and 45-min recovery; probably a typo for 23:25 UTC. Left as written.
- 2025-11-06: judged CLOSED/planned per the rule, although weather alone would have closed the dome; if the analysis prefers a weather attribution for that night, P(weather) could be set to ~0.5.
- AuxTel wind/humidity closures (11-01, 11-02, 11-06, 11-08) are noted as AuxTel statements and do not change the Simonyi status.
- Several nights (11-12, 11-14, 11-26, 11-27) had faults after dome closure that only cost daily calibrations; these are not counted as on-sky loss.
