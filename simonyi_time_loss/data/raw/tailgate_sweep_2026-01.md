- No mention ≠ no weather: notes often summarise the night in one or two lines; an OPEN night with no weather words may still have had short closures.
- Monday notes compress three nights (01-02/03/04, 01-16/17/18, 01-23/24/25, 01-30/31/02-01). For 01-16/17/18 the stated losses (3 h, 1 h 50 m) could not be assigned to a specific night; all three rows carry the same judgement and are flagged "night not identified".
- Hours lost are quoted only when stated in the note (0.75 h, ~1 h, "Almost 7h", 3 h, 1 h 50 m, "only approximately one hour of on-sky", "Three hours of observation", "2h of Feature-Based Scheduler"). No other durations were inferred.
- Ticket-to-event mapping in the 01-19 note is ambiguous (OBS-1527 and OBS-1535 are listed as bare links between bullets); both are listed without assigning which belongs to the hexapod vs. the MTCamera event.
- The 01-12 "no notes" post pointed to an AI-notes canvas; it was not read (single-source rule), so 01-09/10/11 stay UNKNOWN here.
- AuxTel-only weather statements were not propagated to Simonyi (P(weather) ≤ 0.2 in those rows).
- Rows for 01-30 → 02-01 overlap the first days of the February sweep; keep one copy when concatenating.
