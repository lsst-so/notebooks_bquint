## Caveats
- Single source: night reports only; no Slack, Confluence or EFD cross-check in this table.
- Status rule applied: OPEN when the stated loss is ≲1.5 h, PARTIAL above that, "≈ closed" when ≤3 h on sky. Hours are only "≈" estimates from two clock times in the report; −12° twilight assumed at ~00:50 / ~08:50 UTC for the late-start and early-close estimates.
- Dew-point losses in this month are driven by the Dynalene-vs-dew-point condensation rule (threshold shifted during January — see weather_criteria_table.md), not by sky conditions; they are counted as weather.
- Jan 2 FBS end time (05:43 UT) conflicts with the report's own 06:30 UTC note; treated as a typo. Jan 14 "time lost at 08:15" is read as a duration, not a clock time. Jan 16 gives no clock times.
- P(weather) 0.1 on Jan 21–24 reflects the AuxTel humidity closures (Simonyi would likely have lost some time too), not a stated Simonyi cause; Jan 25 raised to 0.5 for the storm/fog.
- For the Q2 per-ticket accounting, the reports give explicit minutes for OBS-1483, OBS-965, OBS-1426, OBS-1515, OBS-1495, OBS-646 (Jan 8), OBS-1493, OBS-1532 (Jan 17), OBS-1535, OBS-1538, OBS-1156.
