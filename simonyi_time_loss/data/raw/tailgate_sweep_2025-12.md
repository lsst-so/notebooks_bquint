## Caveats

- No mention ≠ no weather: notes that say "smooth night" may still hide short humidity holds.
- Monday notes compress three nights; the Dec 9 note compresses four (Fri–Mon, Dec 8 holiday) and the Dec 29 note three. Per-night attribution inside those blocks is a judgement, flagged with [check:].
- Hours lost are quoted only when stated (4 h Nov 30; ~1.3 h Dec 12; 3 h Dec 16; ~1.5 h Dec 17; 0.37 h + ~1.4 h Dec 18). Where the note says "unclear how much time we lost" no number is given.
- Condensation closures (Dec 21, 26–28) are not cleanly weather or fault: the trigger is humidity but the mechanism is cold, uninsulated Dynalene lines. Both probabilities are set high so they can be re-split later against telemetry.
- The Dec 23 note changed to a different format (no nightlog section), so Dec 22 is UNKNOWN even though the condensation discussion suggests the dome was closed.
- The Jan 2 note's single "Nominal night" may belong to Dec 31 or Jan 1; this file does not claim it.
