## Caveats

- No mention ≠ no weather: a note that says nothing about conditions does not mean the night was clear; P(weather)=0 only records that the note gave no weather reason.
- Monday notes compress three nights (and the 2026-05-04 note does not split the weekend at all); per-night status for 2026-05-01/02/03 is inferred from a weekend-level description.
- The 2026-05-22 (Friday) note follows a holiday with no note; its nightlog is not labelled by night and is assigned here to 2026-05-21 only.
- Hours lost are quoted only when the note states them; many notes list faults without a time figure, and PARTIAL vs OPEN for those nights is a judgement.
