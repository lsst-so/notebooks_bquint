| tickets | OBS keys listed in the report. |

## Caveats
- Single source (night reports only); no cross-check with Slack, Confluence or the tailgate notes. Probabilities are judgments, not measurements.
- Hours lost are only estimated where the report gives two clock times (or states a loss explicitly) and are marked "≈"; nominal June night taken as ~22:45–10:45 UTC.
- Jun 1, Jun 15, Jun 16 are mixed planned/weather nights: the telescope would have stayed off sky for camera work regardless of weather, so P(weather) is set at or below 0.5 there and "planned" is noted.
- Jun 9 is labelled "planned downtime due to clouds" by the observers but the day report also says the TMA was locked at horizon for overnight helium sniffing; treated primarily as weather.
- Jun 11 time loss comes from homing difficulties that the report attributes to MTMount behaviour (OBS-1987/1897), not weather, though clouds were present; the 5%-speed observing is noted but not counted as loss.
- Jun 21–24 is one continuous camera (cryo) outage; Jun 25 was the first night back on ~3.5 cryo circuits.
- Jun 7 and Jun 13 power events were control-room/workstation outages (OBS-1944), not observatory-wide glitches; flagged for completeness.
- Ticket keys are copied from the report's ticket list; some (e.g. OBS-1870, OBS-1268, OBS-1985, OBS-1991, OBS-1959, OBS-1962) relate to AuxTel rather than Simonyi.
