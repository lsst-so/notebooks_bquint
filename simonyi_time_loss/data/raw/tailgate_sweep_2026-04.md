## Caveats

- No mention ≠ no weather. The notes report faults far more consistently than weather; a night listed with P(weather)=0 may still have had clouds or wind that nobody wrote down.
- Monday notes compress three nights; in this month two of the four Monday notes (Apr 20, Apr 27) described only a single night, so Fri/Sat nights of those weekends are UNKNOWN rather than merged. The Apr 6 and Apr 13 Monday notes did split by night.
- Hours lost are quoted only when stated in the note. Many PARTIAL nights (e.g. Apr 2–7, Apr 9, Apr 11, Apr 19) list faults without durations; "PARTIAL" there is a judgement that faults cost a block of time, not a measured value.
- The Apr 28 and Apr 29 notes do not explicitly say Simonyi observed; OPEN is inferred from the absence of reported faults/closures.
- "Camera issue" (Apr 26) and "Some TMA issues" (Apr 23) are not tied to ticket keys in the notes.
- Only #summit-announce tailgate threads were read; no other channels, night logs, or Jira were consulted.
