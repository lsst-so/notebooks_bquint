## Summary for this window
- Weather closures/losses: Oct 31 (humidity then overcast; dome closed 23:44 UTC, only the last ~1.5 h on sky). Possible minor weather influence on the Oct 26 early close (clouds in the last hours, not stated as the reason).
- Fault closures: Oct 28 (dome shutter ~3.5 h, Dynalene chiller 01 ~3 h, network crash, M1M3 fault; 30–45 min on sky), Oct 29 (Dynalene emergency shortly after 00:10 UTC, camera powered off, rest of night in-dome; OBS-1310). Minor fault losses: Oct 27 (≈1.1 h, OBS-902/OBS-1304), Oct 30 (≈20 min, OBS-738).
- Neither: Oct 25 (observations canceled for the end-of-Construction celebration — planned).
- Power glitches: none reported.
- UNKNOWN: none (all 7 nights have a report).

## Differences vs the tailgate file (Source 1)
- Oct 25: tailgate CLOSED with no reason ("?/?"); night log gives the reason — celebration, planned → 0/0.
- Oct 26: tailgate PARTIAL fault (0.9) from the chiller/dome/mirror-cover items; night log says those were daytime issues and "All tests run according to plan" → OPEN.
- Oct 27: both OPEN; night log quantifies ≈1.1 h of fault loss (20 + 48 min).
- Oct 28: agree (PARTIAL ≈ closed, faults); night log lists no OBS keys, tailgate has OBS-1309/1310/1312.
- Oct 29: tailgate PARTIAL with on-sky time unstated; night log makes it PARTIAL (≈ closed) — on sky ~00:10 UTC, then in-dome the rest of the night.
- Oct 30: tailgate UNKNOWN (no note on the holiday) → night log OPEN, clear skies, ≈20 min fault loss.
- Oct 31: tailgate OPEN (0.2/0.1, humidity stated for AuxTel only) → night log PARTIAL (≈ closed), weather 1.0. The tailgate inference was wrong.

## Caveats
- Single-source table from the night reports; Slack/Confluence not consulted.
- Oct 26 close at 07:50 UTC: the report does not say whether the early close was planned (end of test list), clouds or the DomeAz fault; low mixed P assigned.
- Oct 28: the dome shutter loss (19:38–23:25 UTC) was mostly daytime/twilight; the ≈3 h Dynalene delay and the network crash are the on-sky losses. The network crash is attributed to IT firewall work — counted as fault, not weather.
- Oct 31: the "last 1.5 hrs" on sky has no clock times in the report; the 23:18/23:44 UTC open/close times are the only ones given.
- Hours lost are "≈" where two clock times allow it; otherwise taken from the report's own estimates ("20 min", "48 minutes", "3-hour delay").

## Summary for this window

- **Weather closures / losses:** none. No night in November was closed or shortened primarily by weather. ## Caveats

- Single-source table from the night reports only; the tailgate (Source 1) file for Oct/Nov 2025 should be cross-checked, in particular 11-03 (cloud degradation vs loss), 11-07 and 11-25 (unquantified delays).
- Hours in "≈" are taken from the reports' own estimates or from two clock times in the report; they are not telemetry-derived. The ≈34 h fault total in the summary is a rough sum of those figures only.
- Status convention used here: OPEN = on sky for the night with interruptions under ~1 h; PARTIAL = a stated loss of ~1 h or more, or an unquantified delay the report calls significant; "PARTIAL (≈ closed)" = nearly no science data. P values for OPEN nights with a quantified sub-hour fault loss are set to 1.0 (attribution), with the magnitude in the evidence; small unquantified interruptions get 0.1–0.5.
- 2025-11-07: the report's "initial alignment ~ 21:25 UTC" is inconsistent with the 23:01 UTC OSS fault and 45-min recovery; probably a typo for 23:25 UTC. Left as written.
- 2025-11-06: judged CLOSED/planned per the rule, although weather alone would have closed the dome; if the analysis prefers a weather attribution for that night, P(weather) could be set to ~0.5.
- AuxTel wind/humidity closures (11-01, 11-02, 11-06, 11-08) are noted as AuxTel statements and do not change the Simonyi status.
- Several nights (11-12, 11-14, 11-26, 11-27) had faults after dome closure that only cost daily calibrations; these are not counted as on-sky loss.

- Neither: Apr 19 (~1 h, visitor demonstrations), Apr 20 (~1 h early close for u-band flats, then cal-system faults).

## Caveats
- Single-source table from the night reports only; Slack, Confluence and tailgate notes were not consulted.
- OPEN vs PARTIAL threshold applied: losses up to ≈1.8 h with the telescope otherwise on sky all night are OPEN; ≥≈2 h are PARTIAL. Apr 24 is PARTIAL on the report's own "6.5 hr if we count this" framing — the telescope was on sky all night, so a data-quality accounting could reclassify it.
- Apr 16–17 downtime was scheduled around the camera vacuum leak repair; P(fault) 0.9 reflects the fault origin rather than planned maintenance. Apr 22 coincided with the planned Cycle 44 deployment, but the report attributes the no-sky to the PXI fault.
- Several reports give loss durations only for some faults (Apr 6, Apr 9, Apr 12): mid probabilities and "ambiguous" are used there.
- Apparent typos in reports left as-is but flagged: Apr 4 "BLOCK-T624 … 23:51 - 23:52 UTC"; Apr 10 "started initial alignment at 21:16 UTC" (likely 23:16).
- Seismic loss (Apr 15, 0.43 h) counted under P(fault), consistent with the July file.

## Caveats
- The five missing reports (May 1–5) cover the first part of the May outage; this table says nothing about those nights — the tailgate file (Source 1) is the only source for them.
- Hours "≈" are derived only where the report gives two clock times; late-start and early-end estimates (May 11, 12, 19, 23, 28, 31) compare against the ~23:10 UTC FBS starts and ~10:35 UTC ends stated on neighbouring nights.
- P(weather)/P(fault) on OPEN nights are scaled to the size of the loss stated in the report (0.05–0.5), as in the other months; they are not shares of the night.
- May 7 is ambiguous between fault and planned work (camera/glycol recovery during a maintenance period); P(fault) 0.9 with the planned component noted.
- May 28's late start mixes a real blocker (OBS-1921, deflector panels) with deliberate alignment/pointing-model configurations; set to mid P(fault) 0.5.
- May 16's early end (camera vacuum, camera-team recommendation) has no clock time in the report, so no hours are given.
- Several reports (May 18, 19) describe power events with no on-sky loss; they are flagged but do not raise the night's status.
- Ticket keys are copied as written in the obs_tickets field; the report text mentions others only in prose (e.g. SUMMIT-12757 on May 21).

## Caveats
- Single source (night reports only); no cross-check with Slack, Confluence or the tailgate notes. Probabilities are judgments, not measurements.
- Hours lost are only estimated where the report gives two clock times (or states a loss explicitly) and are marked "≈"; nominal June night taken as ~22:45–10:45 UTC.
- Jun 1, Jun 15, Jun 16 are mixed planned/weather nights: the telescope would have stayed off sky for camera work regardless of weather, so P(weather) is set at or below 0.5 there and "planned" is noted.
- Jun 9 is labelled "planned downtime due to clouds" by the observers but the day report also says the TMA was locked at horizon for overnight helium sniffing; treated primarily as weather.
- Jun 11 time loss comes from homing difficulties that the report attributes to MTMount behaviour (OBS-1987/1897), not weather, though clouds were present; the 5%-speed observing is noted but not counted as loss.
- Jun 21–24 is one continuous camera (cryo) outage; Jun 25 was the first night back on ~3.5 cryo circuits.
- Jun 7 and Jun 13 power events were control-room/workstation outages (OBS-1944), not observatory-wide glitches; flagged for completeness.
- Ticket keys are copied from the report's ticket list; some (e.g. OBS-1870, OBS-1268, OBS-1985, OBS-1991, OBS-1959, OBS-1962) relate to AuxTel rather than Simonyi.
