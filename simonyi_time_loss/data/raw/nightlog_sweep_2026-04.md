## Caveats
- Single-source table from the night reports only; Slack, Confluence and tailgate notes were not consulted.
- OPEN vs PARTIAL threshold applied: losses up to ≈1.8 h with the telescope otherwise on sky all night are OPEN; ≥≈2 h are PARTIAL. Apr 24 is PARTIAL on the report's own "6.5 hr if we count this" framing — the telescope was on sky all night, so a data-quality accounting could reclassify it.
- Apr 16–17 downtime was scheduled around the camera vacuum leak repair; P(fault) 0.9 reflects the fault origin rather than planned maintenance. Apr 22 coincided with the planned Cycle 44 deployment, but the report attributes the no-sky to the PXI fault.
- Several reports give loss durations only for some faults (Apr 6, Apr 9, Apr 12): mid probabilities and "ambiguous" are used there.
- Apparent typos in reports left as-is but flagged: Apr 4 "BLOCK-T624 … 23:51 - 23:52 UTC"; Apr 10 "started initial alignment at 21:16 UTC" (likely 23:16).
- Seismic loss (Apr 15, 0.43 h) counted under P(fault), consistent with the July file.
