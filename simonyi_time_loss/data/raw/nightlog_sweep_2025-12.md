- Fault closures: Dec 13 (OSS/Chiller 2 unrecoverable, mixed with weather); Dec 26 mixed (dew point + stuck +X mirror cover OBS-1497). Fault partials: Dec 12 (FES, 1.3 h), Dec 16 (GIS reboot, ~3.3 h), Dec 17 (CCW/TMA drives, ~1.5 h), Dec 18 (multiple, ~1.8 h), Dec 19 (M1M3TS, 1 h), Dec 29 (encoder/rotator/MTMount faults), Dec 6 (OSS fault after temperature drop, 45 min), Dec 8 (~20 min TMA).
- Neither (staffing/planned): none explicit. Dec 28 is ambiguous — closed-dome PTC with no stated reason (condensation precaution / planned camera tests / stuck mirror cover), both P at 0.4.
- Power glitches: none reported in December.
- UNKNOWN nights: none — all 31 reports found.
- Condensation era: from Dec 21 the dome-open decision is governed by the dew point vs Dynalene temperature (<2 °C → closed; 20-min rule adopted Dec 22; Chiller setpoint −1 → +1 → +2 °C over Dec 27–30). Dec 21–28 lost most or all of six nights to this.

## Caveats
- Single-source table (night reports only); Slack, Confluence and the tailgate files were not consulted. Cross-check against tailgate_sweep_2025-12.md is a separate step.
- Hours lost are quoted only where the report gives them or two clock times; opening delays on Dec 14, 22 and 24 are not converted to hours because no sunset/−12° time is in the report.
- Condensation closures (Dec 21–28) are weather-triggered but rest on an engineering condition (uninsulated Dynalene lines, setpoint at −1 °C). They are scored mostly weather, with P(fault) 0.2–0.4 to flag the engineering share; adjust if the criteria table treats the Dynalene rule differently.
- PARTIAL threshold used: ≥ ~1 h of stated or clearly implied loss; smaller stated losses (Dec 6, Dec 8) are OPEN/PARTIAL per the report's wording.
- Dec 13 split (0.6/0.4) assumes a ~23:30–08:40 UTC night: humidity until 05:40 UTC, OSS fault for the remainder.
- Dec 5 is marked CLOSED although the dome was physically opened; no on-sky data was obtained ("8hrs lost").
