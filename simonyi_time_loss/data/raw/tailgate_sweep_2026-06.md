## Caveats

- No mention ≠ no weather: a night with no weather words in the note gets P(weather) = 0 only because nothing was said, not because conditions were measured.
- Monday notes compress three nights (and the 06-30 note compresses four, Fri–Mon, because 06-29 was a holiday); where the note gives a weekend-level statement, the same wording is repeated across rows and tickets are marked "day not stated".
- Hours lost are quoted only when stated in the note (06-11 "over an hour", 06-25 "~1.5 hr", 06-30 0.75 h + ~1.65 h + 45 min). No other row has an hours figure.
- Two Monday notes (06-08, 06-22) describe daytime work and faults but not the Friday/Saturday nights; those rows are UNKNOWN rather than inferred.
- 06-07 PARTIAL and 06-16 CLOSED are inferences from indirect wording ("MTCamera to OFFLINE"; pump cart still connected + Nexus block), flagged in the evidence column.
- OBS-1987 appears on 06-11 (MTMount encoder faults) and 06-26 (MTDome Signal A11 GIS / MTVMS); one of the two is probably a typo in the notes — flagged `[check:]`.
- Source is the single channel #summit-announce; no other channel, FRACAS or EFD data was consulted.
